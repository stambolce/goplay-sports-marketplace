## Discover sports. Connect with opportunities.

**[GoPlay](https://goplay.mk)** is a PROCESS IN digital product with a focused mission: Help people find sports activities, opportunities and relevant local or specialized offerings. The product connects its central use case with a recognizable brand experience, practical workflows and a clear path for the people who use it.

It is presented here as a **real product showcase**, not an open-source code repository. The areas below describe the product's functional scope and intended use; individual capabilities, account access and commercial terms should be confirmed through the official product channel.

## How GoPlay works

| Stage | Practical meaning |
|---|---|
| **01 — Explore sports interests and service categories** | Establish the starting context and what success should look like. |
| **02 — Search and compare relevant listings** | Translate the objective into a structured product workflow. |
| **03 — Review provider and activity information** | Bring the relevant content, people or activities into focus. |
| **04 — Identify a suitable offer** | Review information and make the next action understandable. |
| **05 — Contact the provider or follow the published next step** | Move from exploration toward a concrete outcome. |
| **06 — Return to discover new sports opportunities** | Keep the process useful as needs evolve. |
