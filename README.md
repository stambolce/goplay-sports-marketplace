## Discover sports opportunities in one place

**GoPlay** is a PROCESS IN product focused on a digital marketplace for sports-related offerings and services. Its purpose is to help people discover relevant sports opportunities while giving providers a dedicated digital presence.

GoPlay is a **sports marketplace**, distinct from [TeamDesk](https://github.com/stambolce/teamdesk-sports-performance), which focuses on training, coaching and athlete performance.

## Product concept and areas

| Area | Product direction |
|---|---|
| **Sports discovery** | Find sports-related activities and opportunities |
| **Provider profiles** | Help sports providers present their offerings |
| **Service listings** | Organize sports-related services in one place |
| **Categories and search** | Browse offerings by type and relevant criteria |
| **Customer journeys** | Make it easier to move from discovery to provider contact |
| **Marketplace operations** | Supporting tools for managing listings and visibility |

> These describe the product concept and potential platform areas, not an independently verified list of launched features. Payments, booking, specific categories, integrations, pricing and availability must be confirmed with PROCESS IN.

## Designed for

**Sports enthusiasts** looking for relevant opportunities, **sports service providers** seeking online visibility, and **organizations** presenting sports-related activities and services.

## Product principles

- **Discovery first:** Make relevant sports offerings easy to find.
- **Provider visibility:** Present services with clarity and consistent information.
- **Simple journeys:** Minimize friction between interest and provider contact.
- **Flexible marketplace structure:** Support a range of sports categories as the product evolves.
